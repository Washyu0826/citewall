DETAILED ACTION

1. Claim(s) 1, 2, 3 rejected under 35 U.S.C. § 103.
   References: US99500021, US99500012.
   Claims 1-3 are rejected under 35 U.S.C. § 103 as being unpatentable over US99500021 in view of US99500012. The primary reference teaches a GaN HEMT with gate-connected field plate. The secondary reference teaches AlGaN barrier layer thickness in the recited range as a routine design choice. One of ordinary skill in the art would have been motivated to combine these teachings to obtain the claimed device with predictable breakdown voltage improvement.
