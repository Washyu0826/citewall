EUROPEAN PATENT OFFICE
Communication pursuant to Article 94(3) EPC

Application No.: EP9900007
Applicant: Veltris Vision B.V.
Examining Division: Munich
Examiner: P. Lindgren
Date of this communication: 2025-08-12
Reference: EPC94-2025-0007

The examination of the above-identified application has revealed that it does not meet the requirements of the European Patent Convention for the reasons set out below. The applicant is invited to file observations and, where appropriate, amendments within 4 MONTHS of the date of this communication.

The following documents are referred to in this communication:
D1: EP9990019
D2: EP9990020

1. Article 84 EPC (claim 4)
1.1 In claim 4 the feature "the neural network is a convolutional network with a feature pyramid" refers to a parameter without stating how or under which conditions it is measured, so that the skilled person cannot establish whether a given product falls within the claim. The scope of protection is therefore not clearly defined.

2. Article 56 EPC (claims 1, 2, 3)
2.1 Document D1 (EP9990019) is regarded as the closest prior art and discloses a inspection system with all features of claim 1 except the last feature. The objective technical problem is to improve efficiency under varying operating conditions. D2 (EP9990020) addresses the same problem and teaches the missing feature; the skilled person would combine D1 with D2 without inventive skill. Claims 2 and 3 add routine design choices.

The application as filed contains 8 claims.
