(54) Method for training a neural network for defect detection with synthetic images

(57) Abstract
A inspection system comprising a camera (132) capturing images of printed circuit boards; a generator (134) producing synthetic defect images by inserting rendered defects into defect-free images; the invention raises recall on rare defect classes from 71 % to 93 % at unchanged false-call rate.

Description
Technical field
The present invention relates to machine-vision inspection of printed circuit boards, and in particular to a inspection system and a corresponding method.

Background art
A first known approach uses training on real defect images only; however, it lacks examples of rare defect classes. A second known approach relies on simple copy-paste augmentation, which produces visible seams that the network learns as a shortcut. Neither approach provides reliable operation over the full range of operating conditions at acceptable cost.

Summary of the invention
It is an object of the invention to provide a inspection system overcoming these drawbacks. The object is achieved by a inspection system comprising a camera (132) capturing images of printed circuit boards; a generator (134) producing synthetic defect images by inserting rendered defects into defect-free images; a neural network (136) trained on real and synthetic images; wherein the generator adjusts rendered defect geometry so that a discriminator cannot distinguish synthetic from real defect patches. Compared with the prior art, the invention raises recall on rare defect classes from 71 % to 93 % at unchanged false-call rate.

Detailed description of embodiments
The generator (134) renders 20,000 synthetic images per defect class; training uses 8 GPUs for 12 hours. In a variant, the defects comprise solder bridges, missing components and tombstoning; in a further variant, the ratio of synthetic to real training images is between 1:1 and 5:1; in a further variant, the neural network is a convolutional network with a feature pyramid. The features of the embodiments may be combined unless they are technically incompatible.

Claims
1. A inspection system comprising: a camera (132) capturing images of printed circuit boards; a generator (134) producing synthetic defect images by inserting rendered defects into defect-free images; a neural network (136) trained on real and synthetic images; wherein the generator adjusts rendered defect geometry so that a discriminator cannot distinguish synthetic from real defect patches.
2. The inspection system according to claim 1, wherein the defects comprise solder bridges, missing components and tombstoning.
3. The inspection system according to claim 1, wherein the ratio of synthetic to real training images is between 1:1 and 5:1.
4. The inspection system according to claim 1, wherein the neural network is a convolutional network with a feature pyramid.
5. The inspection system according to claim 1, wherein the system flags boards whose defect probability exceeds 0.8 for manual review.
6. The inspection system according to claim 2 or 3, wherein the camera captures images under three illumination angles.
7. A computer-implemented method of training a neural network for defect detection, comprising: rendering defects into defect-free images of printed circuit boards; adjusting rendered defect geometry until a discriminator cannot distinguish synthetic from real defect patches; training the neural network on real and synthetic images.
8. The method according to claim 7, wherein the defects comprise solder bridges, missing components and tombstoning.
