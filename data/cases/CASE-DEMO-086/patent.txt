(54) Coated cutting insert with multilayer aluminium oxide coating

(57) Abstract
A cutting insert comprising a cemented carbide substrate (112); a titanium carbonitride inner layer (114) with a thickness of 4 µm to 10 µm; the invention increases tool life in turning of 42CrMo4 at 300 m/min by approximately 45 %.

Description
Technical field
The present invention relates to coated cemented carbide inserts for metal turning, and in particular to a cutting insert and a corresponding method.

Background art
A first known approach uses alumina coatings with random texture; however, it suffer crater wear at high cutting speeds. A second known approach relies on (0 0 1)-textured coatings with thin inner layers, which show flaking in interrupted cuts. Neither approach provides reliable operation over the full range of operating conditions at acceptable cost.

Summary of the invention
It is an object of the invention to provide a cutting insert overcoming these drawbacks. The object is achieved by a cutting insert comprising a cemented carbide substrate (112); a titanium carbonitride inner layer (114) with a thickness of 4 µm to 10 µm; an alpha-alumina outer layer (116) with a thickness of 3 µm to 8 µm; wherein the alpha-alumina layer exhibits a texture coefficient TC(0 0 12) of at least 6. Compared with the prior art, the invention increases tool life in turning of 42CrMo4 at 300 m/min by approximately 45 %.

Brief description of the drawings
Fig. 1 shows a schematic view of a cutting insert according to a first embodiment. Fig. 2 shows a flow chart of the method. Fig. 3 shows measurement results comparing the embodiment with the prior art.

Detailed description of embodiments
Example 1 uses a 7 µm Ti(C,N) layer and a 5 µm alumina layer with TC(0 0 12) = 7.4 measured by XRD. In a variant, the texture coefficient TC(0 0 12) is at least 7; in a further variant, a bonding layer (118) of titanium oxycarbonitride is arranged between the inner and outer layers; in a further variant, the outer layer is subjected to wet blasting to a residual compressive stress of at least 1 GPa. The features of the embodiments may be combined unless they are technically incompatible.

Claims
1. A cutting insert comprising: a cemented carbide substrate (112); a titanium carbonitride inner layer (114) with a thickness of 4 µm to 10 µm; an alpha-alumina outer layer (116) with a thickness of 3 µm to 8 µm; wherein the alpha-alumina layer exhibits a texture coefficient TC(0 0 12) of at least 6.
2. The cutting insert according to claim 1, wherein the texture coefficient TC(0 0 12) is at least 7.
3. The cutting insert according to claim 1, wherein a bonding layer (118) of titanium oxycarbonitride is arranged between the inner and outer layers.
4. The cutting insert according to claim 1, wherein the outer layer is subjected to wet blasting to a residual compressive stress of at least 1 GPa.
5. The cutting insert according to claim 1, wherein the substrate contains 5 wt % to 7 wt % cobalt.
6. The cutting insert according to claim 2 or 3, wherein a titanium nitride top layer is removed from the rake face.
7. A method of producing a coated cutting insert, comprising: depositing a titanium carbonitride layer by MT-CVD at 850 °C to 900 °C; depositing an alpha-alumina layer by CVD at 1000 °C with a controlled CO2 ratio; wet blasting the coated insert.
8. The method according to claim 7, wherein the texture coefficient TC(0 0 12) is at least 7.
